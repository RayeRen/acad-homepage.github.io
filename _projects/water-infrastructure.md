## Current role

I am an M.S. student and Graduate Research Assistant working with Dr. Ram Krishna Mazumder. My current work combines predictive modeling, risk assessment, and water infrastructure asset management. The directions below describe ongoing research objectives, not completed findings.

## Automated sewer condition assessment

**Question:** How can defects identified in sewer CCTV imagery become useful condition information for rehabilitation planning?

The work investigates computer-vision methods for defect identification and the connection between image-level predictions and asset assessment. The sewer project is a collaboration with Julker Nayen Rafi and Dr. Mazumder, as listed in my CV. Model development and analysis are in progress.

## Water-network performance and resilience

**Question:** What do complementary measures reveal about the performance of a water distribution system?

The research considers network structure, hydraulic performance, water security, and economic performance. The goal is to support a broader view of reliability and improvement priorities. Analysis is in progress; no ranking or performance result is presented here.

## Water infrastructure affordability in Georgia

**Question:** How does water infrastructure affordability vary across communities?

This work examines GEFA-based assessment and household income information, with attention to geographic and socioeconomic variation. Analysis is ongoing. The geographic unit and indicator definitions matter: community-level indicators should not automatically be interpreted as individual household burdens.
