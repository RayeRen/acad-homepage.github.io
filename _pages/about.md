# 👋 About Me

Halo, this is Henryk Chan (Haolin Chen), a passionate researcher and medical engineering innovator committed to advancing healthcare through cutting-edge biomedical technologies. With a solid academic foundation—holding an accredited engineering degree and currently pursuing a master’s program, both recognized by the Institution of Engineering and Technology (IET)—my work bridges engineering excellence and clinical impact.

My research centers on wearable electrochemical biosensors, wound-monitoring biosensors, and ECG systems leveraging liquid metal technology. I have authored several peer-reviewed articles published in top-tier SCI Q1 journals with total <a href='https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=SCHOLAR_ID&user=Yu9lHQgAAAAJ'><img src="https://img.shields.io/endpoint?url={{ url | url_encode }}&logo=Google%20Scholar&labelColor=f6f6f6&color=9cf&style=flat&label=citations"></a>. Additionally, and hold a certified patent for an innovative device, underscoring my drive to transform research into practical, real-world solutions.

In addition to my academic and research pursuits, I engage in interdisciplinary innovation as a member of the Advanced Hackspace team, where I contribute to the development of creative engineering tools and prototypes. I also volunteer as a Responder at Hammersmith Hospital (Imperial College Healthcare NHS Trust), supporting frontline staff and contributing to patient-centered care.
