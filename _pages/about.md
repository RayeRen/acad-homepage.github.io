I am a Biomedical Engineer with an MSc in Intelligent Systems and a Data Scientist at the **Artificial Intelligence Unit of Fundación Valle del Lili** (Cali, Colombia) and a collaborator of **MIT Critical Data**. My work focuses on the application of Artificial Intelligence in healthcare, developing data-driven solutions to improve clinical decision-making and patient outcomes.

I am passionate about bridging the gap between cutting-edge AI research and real-world medical applications, working at the intersection of machine learning, clinical data, and biomedical engineering.
