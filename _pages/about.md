<span class='anchor' id='about-me'></span>


I am currently a third-year Ph.D. student in the [School of Integrated Circuits (School of Information Science and Electronic Engineering)](https://icisee.sjtu.edu.cn/) at Shanghai Jiao Tong University of China, where I am supervised by [Prof. Changzhan Gu](https://changzhan.sjtu.edu.cn/index.html). 

I have authored or co-authored more than 20 journal and conference papers, including more than 10 as the first author. I received the IEEE Microwave Theory and Technology Society (MTT-S) Undergraduate/Pre-graduate Scholarship and the National Scholarship for Graduate Students in China. I also won third place in the 2024 IEEE MTT-S International Wireless Symposium (IWS) MVC Competition, received the Best Paper Award at the 2025 Cross Strait Radio Science and Wireless Technology Conference (CSRSWTC), and won second place in the Best Student Paper category at the 2026 IEEE MTT-S Shanghai Postgraduate Paper Competition on Frontiers in Electronics and Information Innovation.

My research interests include millimeter-wave radar systems, full-duplex antennas, and heterogeneous integration and packaging technologies.

Analysis and Experiments on the Impact of Frequency Nonlinearity on Displacement Motion Sensing With FMCW Radar
</a>
</p>

**Zhiwei Zhang**, Jingtao Liu, Jiayu Zhang, Yijing Guo and Changzhan Gu* <br>
***IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques, 2024*** <br>
- This paper presents the first investigation into the influence of NLFM on displacement motion sensing, supported by theoretical derivations, simulation, and experimental validations.
- Building on demonstrating motion‐sensing robustness against NLFM, a VCO in open-loop FMCW radar architecture is validated for displacement motion sensing. Comparative experiments show that, despite reduced hardware complexity, this architecture achieves performance on par with VCO in closed‐loop FMCW radar, with normalized root mean square error (NRMSE) difference below 1.6%.

**Zhiwei Zhang**, Jiayu Zhang, Jingtao Liu, Changzhan Gu* and Junfa Mao <br>
***IEEE Transactions on Microwave Theory and Techniques, 2024*** <br>
- This technology can be applied to enhance motion sensing in millimeter-wave radar.
- Using equivalent FMCW radar technology, the equivalent signal wavelength can be arbitrarily increased or decreased to achieve precise demodulation of large or small displacements. This improves the accuracy of small displacement detection by three times and the accuracy of large displacement detection by ten times.
